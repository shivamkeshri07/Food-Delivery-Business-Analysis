# 🍔 Food Delivery Business Analysis — MySQL

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze food delivery business operations and solve real-world business problems using **MySQL and Advanced SQL**.

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer Acquisition
- Customer Retention
- Restaurant Performance
- Promotional Behavior
- Customer Engagement
- Organic Customer Growth

---

## 💼 Business Problems Covered

This project addresses the following real-world business questions:

- Which are the **Top 3 outlets within each cuisine category** based on order volume?
- How many **new customers are acquired each day**?
- Which customers were acquired in **January 2025**, placed only one order, and did not return?
- Which customers are **inactive**, were acquired more than one month ago, and used a promo code on their first order?
- Which customers should be targeted after **every third order** for personalized communication?
- Which customers have placed multiple orders and used a **promo code on every order**?
- What percentage of customers were **organically acquired** without using a promotional code?

---

## 💡 Business Insights & Outcomes

The analysis provides insights into key areas of food delivery business performance:

- **Restaurant Performance:** Identifies the top-performing outlets within each cuisine category based on order volume.
- **Customer Acquisition:** Tracks daily new customer acquisition and provides visibility into customer growth.
- **Customer Retention:** Identifies one-time customers who did not return, highlighting opportunities for retention campaigns.
- **Customer Inactivity:** Identifies inactive customers who can be targeted through re-engagement strategies.
- **Promotional Behavior:** Highlights customers who consistently rely on promotional codes, supporting better discount and campaign analysis.
- **Customer Engagement:** Identifies customer order milestones that can be used for personalized communication and loyalty initiatives.
- **Organic Growth:** Measures customer acquisition without promotional incentives to understand organic customer growth.

Overall, this project demonstrates the ability to translate **business requirements into advanced SQL analysis** and generate insights that can support **customer growth, retention, marketing, restaurant performance, and engagement strategies**.
